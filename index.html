Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a keratin treatment last?

Results vary with hair type, lifestyle and aftercare, but a keratin treatment generally lasts for a few months and fades gradually. Your stylist will explain what to expect for your hair.


What is the difference between keratin and a Brazilian blowout?

Both are smoothing treatments that reduce frizz and add shine. Products and formulas differ, so our stylists will recommend the one that suits your hair texture and your goals.


Can I get highlights or haircuts at the same visit?

Yes. We offer cuts, balayage and highlights, and your stylist can advise on the best order and timing so that each service gives its best result.

Keratin Treatment

A keratin treatment is applied section by section, sealed into the hair with heat and then finished so that hair dries smoother and more manageable. The effect is a sleek, shiny finish that does not need constant straightening.

We choose the approach based on your hair's length, thickness and condition, and we show you how to wash and care for it afterward so you get the longest-lasting result.
